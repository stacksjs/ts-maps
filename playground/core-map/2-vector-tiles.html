Vector tiles

The zero-dependency vector tile pipeline: .pbf tiles decoded by the in-house protobuf reader and drawn through style layers that pick a source layer, a draw type and paint. Click the map to queryRenderedFeatures; the hits appear in the panel. The tile is made in the browser; add ?tiles=https://your/{z}/{x}/{y}.pbf to point at a real server.
