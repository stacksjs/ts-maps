10. Street labels & theming

Road names drawn with symbol-placement: 'line' — each glyph rotated to the tangent of the road it names, dropped when the line bends past text-max-angle, and repeated every symbol-spacing pixels. Place names use point placement with text-anchor and text-offset, and outrank street names via symbol-sort-key. One collision index spans every tile, so nothing doubles up across a seam. The toggle flips the map style, the map chrome, and the page together.
