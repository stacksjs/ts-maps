Data & layers

Vector tiles

The zero-dependency vector tile pipeline: .pbf tiles decoded by the in-house protobuf reader and drawn through style layers that pick a source layer, a draw type and paint.

Labels & symbols

A made-up vector tile with 20 named places, drawn by one symbol layer.

Street labels & theming

Road names follow their roads with symbol-placement: 'line', each glyph turned to the curve and dropped where it bends past text-max-angle.

Raster & heatmap

An OpenStreetMap raster basemap under a HeatmapLayer of 800 points round five spots in Manhattan.

Clustering & feature-state

400 points round Manhattan, clustered in the browser by GeoJSONClusterSource (a zero-dependency supercluster port).

3D & rendering

Landmarks & trees

The Transamerica Pyramid as a glTF model standing in for its building, and trees planted in the Presidio's woods once the map tilts.

3D camera, sky & fog

Tilt with setPitch, turn with setBearing, colour the sky with setSky and soften the horizon with setFog, over the vector basemap's 3D buildings.

Globe

The globe projection: a sphere when zoomed out, Mercator past zoom 6, crossing over smoothly at 5.5.

WebGL renderer

The same vector tile through the Canvas2D or the WebGL backend.

Territory capture

A simulated runner sends GPS positions; LoopDetector notices when the path closes, TerritoryStore merges the claim and takes the overlap from rivals, and TerritoryLayer draws it.
